📍 Centre

The centre is the exact middle point of a circle. All points on the circle boundary are at equal distance from the centre!

Change the radius and watch what happens to the diameter!

Live Formula Diameter = 2 × Radius Diameter = 2 × 5 cm = 10 cm
Reverse Formula Radius = Diameter ÷ 2 Radius = 10 cm ÷ 2 = 5 cm
